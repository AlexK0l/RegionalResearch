# Saby: fleet coverage and speed are separate release criteria

Production evidence supplied 2026-11-26 (events, not unique companies):
Vologda's old sweep hit its 30-minute cap. Karelia's four-pass sweep completed
1002 tasks but verified positive fleets for only 121 of 404 direct buyers.
Karelia logs include 164 missing fleet headings, 101 missing truck previews,
6 incomplete vehicle lists, 363 private-route-change diagnostics and 189
uncertain zero-result searches. Many alias diagnostics may be followed by
successful proof; do not sum these as distinct failed companies.

This batch improves both axes without accepting weaker transport or identity
evidence:

- Scan exact INN text before visibility/geometry checks and compile the
  regular expression once per search DOM snapshot. Keep all result-card,
  branch, ambiguity, row-limit and identity gates. The fixture with 1500
  unrelated nodes proves zero geometry/style reads for those nodes.
- Run the existing opt-in, independently verified post-route proof early
  after its narrowly eligible route transition and a FRESH scoped card
  assessment. On failure retain the original bounded card hydration and
  final proof. No route-change whitelist is expanded.
- On a full fleet visit, recover a late/unmounted exact heading with a short
  passive poll and a bounded sweep of the unique contractor scroll wrapper
  observed in the real finance HTML. Do not click or scroll the body, an
  ambiguous viewer or a changed contractor. Ready headings do not incur
  recovery waits. Restore the original scroll on unsuccessful recovery.
- A uniquely observed positive truck tile whose preview parser produces
  excess text leaves can start the existing strict full Wasaby tree read;
  discard ALL suspect preview rows. Only full exact-count model evidence
  completes the fleet. Quick/legacy preview validation and explicit zero
  rejection are unchanged. No new guessed labels or pagination controls.
- Record safe machine-readable section failure codes and full fleet
  outcomes (INN, phase, fixed status/reason); quick reads are not failures
  of the full collector. Keep existing rotation/admission, per-task
  cursor, no global deadline and contact priority.

The new navigation paths are supported by offline fixtures; real coverage
and elapsed-time gains are NOT yet demonstrated. No change to XLSX layout,
Saby login, API budgets, saved checkpoints or regional results. This batch
does not yet extend the fleet scope to leasing/intermediaries (the strict
template has no fleet column in those sheets), recover missing INNs, or
solve every uncertain result-grid/route transition. Do not launch a second
browser or restart active Tver. After deployment use a small matched
problem-card cohort and compare: exact full fleets, positive fleets,
unresolved distinct INNs by cause, total visits, full searches, per-visit
elapsed time and complete fleets/hour. A faster run with lost verified
coverage is a regression; more coverage by unconstrained retries is not
an acceptable speed result either.
